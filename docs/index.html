Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Zevqo Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CertAlert mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use CertAlert.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary for the App to function:

  • Account information: name, email address, and organization details you provide during registration.
  • Certificate/permit data: names, expiry dates, issuing authorities, and related files you choose to upload or enter.
  • Device information: basic device identifiers used for push notifications and app functionality.
  • Usage data: anonymous usage patterns to improve the App.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as credit card numbers, government IDs, or health data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain certificate expiry tracking.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have opted into.
  • To improve App functionality and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored securely using Supabase with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Push notification tokens are stored securely for delivering reminders.
  • Files you upload are stored securely and are only accessible within your organization.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only:

  • With service providers who assist in App operation (hosting, notifications).
  • When required by law or to protect our rights and safety.
  • With your explicit consent.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your data within the App.
  • Correction: You can update your information in the App settings.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and associated data.
  • Export: You can export your certificate data at any time.

Children's Privacy

CertAlert is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email.
